Day 1 • Sept 15
Arrival Kraków & Travel to Ždiar
Pickup rented car at Kraków Airport. Drive south through Poland into Slovakia to Ždiar village. Rest at B&B.
🚗 ~2h Drive🏡 Stay: Ždiar B&B
Day 2 • Sept 16
Ždiar to Chata pri Zelenom plese
Start trekking along Tatranská Magistrála trail entering Belianske Tatras up to Emerald Lake mountain hut.
🥾 ~11 km⛰️ +750 m ascent🏡 Stay: Zelenom plese
Day 3 • Sept 17
Zelenom plese to Zbojnícka Chata
Ascend steep zigzag trail to Veľká Svišťovka pass (2,037m). Traverse past Skalnaté Pleso to Zbojnícka chata.
🥾 ~13 km⛰️ High pass crossing🏡 Stay: Zbojnícka Chata
Day 4 • Sept 18
Zbojnícka Chata to Popradské Pleso
Cross chain-assisted Prielom pass (2,288m) into Veľká Studená dolina down towards Popradské pleso lake.
🥾 ~12 km⛓️ Chains at Prielom🏡 Stay: Popradské Pleso

Hike down to vehicle parking. Drive to Terma Bania thermal baths for muscle recovery before driving to Kraków.
